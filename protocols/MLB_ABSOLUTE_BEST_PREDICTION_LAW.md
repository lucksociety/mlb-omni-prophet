Before outputting ANY prediction, the Agent MUST:

  - Probability Over [Line]: XX.X%

### IV. FINAL SCORE PREDICTIONS
- **Away**: X.XX [p15 — p85] runs | **Win Prob**: XX.X%

### V. HOME RUN TRACKER (>25% PROBABILITY)
- If any player appears in > 25,000 of 100,000 HR simulations:
  - **[Player Name] ([Team])**: XX.X% Probability
- If no players exceed 25%, state "No high-probability HR targets detected."

## 3. PROBABILITY LAW
Analytical distributions (Poisson, Normal, etc.) are **FORBIDDEN**. 
- **Example**: If Ohtani gets 7+ strikeouts in 62,341 out of 100,000 runs, the probability is **62.3%**. 
- You MUST report the raw frequency from the Monte Carlo results.
